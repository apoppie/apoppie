## Aaron Poppie

Captain of Lightcone Studios. I build AI systems where different kinds of minds work together, and the institutions for the people building alongside them. Seattle, WA.

I work mostly in the open through two efforts.

### AI Northwest

The Pacific Northwest's AI builder community. Events, a forum, and the infrastructure that runs it.

- [ainw-forum-skills](https://github.com/ainorthwest/ainw-forum-skills). Agent skills for participating in a community forum. Works with any Agent Skills-standard harness.
- [ainw-kiosk](https://github.com/ainorthwest/ainw-kiosk). Check-in at the door. A QR scan looks the guest up in Luma, then records attendance in HubSpot.
- [ainw-digest](https://github.com/ainorthwest/ainw-digest). The weekly forum digest email. A Cloudflare Worker.

### Lightcone Studios

The studio. Most of the product work is private, but the fun lives out here.

- [hummingbird-cam](https://github.com/lightcone-studios/hummingbird-cam). A Raspberry Pi nest monitor I built for a family of rescued hummingbirds.
- [intelligence-mission-control](https://github.com/apoppie/intelligence-mission-control). A NASA-styled autonomous intel-gathering console, powered by Claude Code.

Currently working on solutions architecture, multi-agent engineering, and figuring out how humans and AI actually register on each other.
